Ishaara इशारा

A silent seat-to-conductor alert for women facing harassment on a bus. This working prototype runs the full loop: passenger, conductor and depot.

Single-screen demo
  1. 1Passenger signals. Tap the seat sticker, send an SMS, or ask the conductor for “Sakhi stop”.
  2. 2Only the conductor’s phone buzzes. It shows her seat zone and a response script that looks like routine duty.
  3. 3Depot sees the pattern. Every alert is logged without names, building a map of risky stretches and hours.
